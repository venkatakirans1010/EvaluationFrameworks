### High-Level Design – Fund Transfer

Frontend:
- TransferFundsPage form
- SummaryPage for confirmation
- SuccessPage displays receipt

Backend:
- POST /api/funds/transfer
  Request: { from_acc, to_acc, amount, otp }
  Response: { status, transaction_id, message }
- Integrations: CoreBankingService, OTPService
- DB Tables: accounts, transactions, otp_logs
