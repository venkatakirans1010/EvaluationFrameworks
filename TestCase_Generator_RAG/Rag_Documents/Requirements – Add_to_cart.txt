### Functional Requirements – Add to Cart

1. The "Add to Cart" button must be available on both product list and product detail pages.
2. When a user clicks "Add to Cart," the product and quantity (default 1) are stored in the cart session.
3. The cart should persist until the user manually clears it or completes checkout.
4. Multiple quantities of the same product should merge under one item with updated quantity.
5. Cart count should display dynamically on the navigation bar.
6. System should validate stock before adding an item.
7. Cart should be saved in database for logged-in users; session storage for guests.
