### Low-Level Design – Leave Module

LeaveService:
- apply_leave(emp_id, leave_type, start_date, end_date, reason)
- validate_balance(emp_id, leave_type)
- route_to_manager(emp_id)

DB:
- leave_requests(id, emp_id, type, start, end, status)
- leave_balances(emp_id, type, balance)

UI Validations:
- Prevent selecting past dates.
- Auto-calculate duration.
